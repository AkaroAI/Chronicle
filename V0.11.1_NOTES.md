# Chronicle v0.11.1 — Starbound Repair

## Canon and narration lifecycle

- Separates the turn into a visible player-input scan, draft generation, and completed-scene scan.
- Holds drafts that introduce canon proposals out of the permanent transcript until Review resolves them.
- Regenerates the final response from resolved canon after any approval, rejection, or edited proposal.
- Prevents simultaneous approval/rejection callbacks from generating duplicate assistant replies.
- Rejects leaked model artifacts such as `</think>`, `dm_commentary`, and structured proposal wrappers.

## DM conversation

- Fixes the native Chronicle Engine payload so DM system instructions and user messages reach Ollama.
- Treats both explicit DM mode and `Talking to → DM` as non-canonical DM conversation.
- Keeps DM conversation out of story history, canonical scans, and structured story payloads.

## Character movement and proposals

- Understands explicit separation such as “Yuki leaves Asira to go…” and moves only Yuki.
- Retains independent character locations and location-note history.
- Adds regression tests for solo movement subjects, paired interactions, DM routing, and native payload preservation.

## High-fidelity Living Chronicle design

- Adds a deterministic starfield with violet and teal nebula lighting.
- Adds translucent raised midnight panels, rim lighting, deeper shadows, and fantasy-serif headings.
- Refines floating navigation and glass surfaces across every main category.
- Adds five original illustrated Chronicle Spirits: happy, confused, sleepy, worried, and watchful.
- Routes proposal notices to the confused Spirit, offline failures to sleepy, errors to worried,
  review warnings to watchful, and successful actions to happy.
