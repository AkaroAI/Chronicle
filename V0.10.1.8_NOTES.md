# Chronicle v0.10.1.8

## Canon reliability pass

- Detects canon-changing player statements before narration and pauses the story for Review.
- Resumes the pending story turn only after its proposals are approved or rejected.
- Repairs missing-ID character updates into minimal character-sheet proposals.
- Prevents unsupported campaign fields and empty approvals from silently succeeding.
- Stores explicit campaign/world years as approved Canon memory.
- Removes narrator-invented location descriptions from player-confirmed proposals.
- Preserves existing location values when a proposal leaves optional fields blank.
- Tightens storyteller rules around player agency, unrequested movement, clues, encounters, and quest progression.
