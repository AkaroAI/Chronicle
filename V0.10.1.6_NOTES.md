# Chronicle v0.10.1.6 — Structured Engine Context

Chronicle now owns and supplies campaign memory explicitly instead of asking the local model to
reconstruct it from a long chat transcript.

- Sends typed campaign, character, memory, location, faction, quest, and timeline context to
  Chronicle Engine's native endpoint.
- Marks the selected player character so the Engine can preserve player agency.
- Limits immediate conversational history to five messages.
- Runs proposal analysis over the completed player + storyteller exchange.
- Proposes character sheets for meaningful named participants who do not have records yet.
- Keeps every AI suggestion inert until the player approves it in Review.

Requires Chronicle Engine v0.3.1 or newer.
