# Chronicle v0.10.1 — Character Presence

## Character presence on the map
World Explorer location nodes can now surface named characters whose canonical records explicitly establish that they are at that location.

Location detail sheets now include a `Characters here` section.

## Independent character positions
The continuity analyzer now treats character position independently from campaign current location.

Examples:
- Asira and Yuki travel to Moonfall Academy.
- Lyra remains in Frostmere.

Chronicle should propose character updates that preserve:
- Asira: `Currently at Moonfall Academy.`
- Yuki: `Currently at Moonfall Academy.`
- Lyra: `Currently at Frostmere.`

The World Explorer reads those approved canonical notes and shows the characters at the appropriate location.

## Safety rules
- Do not teleport every character with the active party.
- Do not infer NPC position from historical association alone.
- Staying behind preserves the previous location.
- Explicit movement is evaluated per character.

## Why dedicated route tables are NOT in this patch
A database-v6 route/presence schema was prototyped, but intentionally held back from the shipping patch.
v0.10.1 keeps database schema 5 and backup v3 so we can validate character-presence behavior without risking campaign migration/import stability.

Dedicated editable routes/connections remain planned for the next World Explorer patch after this character-location layer passes.
